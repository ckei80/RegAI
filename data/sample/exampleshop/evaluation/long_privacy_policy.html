Privacy Policy

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. Your email address is required to create and access your account.

Orders and Payments

We retain order information so that you can review your purchase history and so that we can process returns and refunds.

Payment transactions are processed by our payment service provider. We do not store complete payment card numbers on our systems.

Why We Use Your Personal Data

We use your personal data to provide the services you request, manage your account, process orders, and provide customer support.

We use your email address to send account-related notifications, including order confirmations and password-reset messages.

Marketing Communications

We may send promotional emails to customers who have opted in to receive marketing communications. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

Data Retention

We retain account information while your account remains active. Some order records may be retained after account closure where necessary for accounting and other applicable record-keeping obligations.

Sharing Information

We share relevant information with service providers that help us operate the website, process payments, and deliver customer support.

Your Rights

You can request access to your personal data, ask us to correct inaccurate information, or request deletion where applicable.
